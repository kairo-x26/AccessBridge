# Patient text messaging policy

## Consent
Text messages are sent only to patients who signed the messaging consent form. The form number is recorded as the consent reference.

## Content
Messages are administrative: scheduling, reminders and offers of help. Never include diagnoses, test results, medications or the reason for the referral.

## Language
Messages are sent in the patient's preferred language using a reviewed template. If no reviewed template exists in that language, the navigator calls the patient with an interpreter instead.

## Opt-out
A patient who replies STOP receives no further texts. Staff can restore texts only when the patient asks in writing.

## Medical questions
If a patient replies with a medical question or symptoms, do not answer by text. Route the message to clinical staff the same day. If the reply suggests an emergency, the nurse on duty calls the patient immediately.

## Transportation and cost
When a patient asks for help with a ride or cost, the navigator contacts them within one business day and documents the outcome on the task.
