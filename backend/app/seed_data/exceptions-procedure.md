# Handling referral exceptions

## Identity mismatch
If the documents describe a different patient than the record (name or date of birth), stop. Confirm identity with the referring clinic before any package is sent. Never merge records to make a mismatch go away.

## Transmission failure
If the exchange cannot deliver a package, check the specialist office's address in the destination list, correct it, move the referral back to review and approve the package again.

## Closing a referral
A referral is closed as completed only with the specialist's consult note or written confirmation that the visit happened. A scheduled appointment or a patient's text saying they went is not enough.

## Corrections
Only administrators can reopen a closed referral. The reason is recorded and the original outcome stays in the history.
