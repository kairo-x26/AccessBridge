# Specialty referral procedure

Riverbend Community Health — administrative procedure (synthetic example)

## Who owns a referral
Every referral is assigned to a referral coordinator within one business day of import. If the coordinator is out, the team lead reassigns open referrals the same morning.

## Required documents
A referral package needs the signed referral form, current insurance details including the member ID, and the most recent visit note. Orthopedics also needs the imaging report. Behavioral health needs signed consent to share records before anything is sent.

## Missing insurance
If the patient has no insurance on file, the coordinator asks the patient navigator to check eligibility for the sliding fee program before the package is approved. Do not send a package without either insurance details or a sliding fee determination.

## Sending a package
Packages are sent through the secure referral exchange only. Fax is used only when the specialist office has no exchange address, and the fax confirmation page must be uploaded as evidence.

## Follow-up timing
If the specialist office has not acknowledged a referral within 5 business days, the coordinator calls the office. After two unanswered follow-ups, the referral is flagged as an exception and the team lead decides next steps.

## Urgent referrals
Urgency is set only by the referring clinician. Coordinators never change it. Referrals the clinician marked urgent are worked first and followed up after 2 business days instead of 5.
